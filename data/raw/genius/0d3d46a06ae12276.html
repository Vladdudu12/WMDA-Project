About
Song Bio

Carly Rae Jepsen of “Call Me Maybe” fame joins Owl City on this radio-friendly comeback single, which peaked at #8 on the Billboard Hot 100.

In October 2012, Jepsen and Adam Young (the man behind Owl City) were sued by Allyson Burnett, who claimed that they had copied her song, “Ah, It’s a Love Song”, which was released in 2010.

It was ruled that “Good Time” did not copy Burnett’s song.

Q&A

Find answers to frequently asked questions about the song and explore its deeper meaning

What did Owl City & Carly Rae Jepsen say about "Good Time"?
Genius Answer

Adam Young described this song as a “happy accident. "I feel like every artist is inherently granted one shot to sing about having a good time in his or her life, and six months ago, I realized I hadn’t cashed in my chips yet,” Young said of creating the single.

Carly said that this song takes her to memories of “Camping with my family and friends in the summer.” “Best times of my life,” Jepsen said.

How did both artists end up making a collaboration?
Genius Answer

According to Songfacts, Adam one said in an unquoted question:

I love Carly’s song ‘Call Me Maybe.’ I remember hearing it for the first time and immediately became a huge fan of hers – it is absolutely contagious. A bit later, I actually found out that she had known of my music for a while and even went to a couple of shows of mine in Vancouver so I definitely knew I wanted to collaborate with her.
