About
Verified Bio

“New Rules” is about moving on from an ex and how you need to stay away from them. The narrator needs to treat themselves like they would a friend with their own advice.

The music video premiered on July 7, 2017. After seeing covers of the song and recreations, Dua Lipa was inspired to release a new video with a compilation of the covers. At the end, she expressed her gratitude:

Thank you so much for being a massive part of ‘#DuasNewRules.’ It feels like yesterday I was uploading covers on YouTube, and now to have all of you guys covering me is absolutely everything. You guys are the best.

“New Rules” became Dua Lipa’s first number one single in the United Kingdom and Ireland.

“New Rules” peaked at #6 on Billboard’s Hot 100 Chart, marking Dua Lipa’s first top-10 hit in America. On July 3rd, 2018, “New Rules” ended it’s 48 week run on the Billboard Hot 100, making it her first single to spend the most weeks on the Hot 100.

What inspired you to make this song and why?
Genius Answer

In the music video’s behind the scenes, Dua said:

It’s the breakup song that I wish I had when I was breaking up with someone. This is me taking charge… It’s about keeping your distance from someone who’s bad for you. I’m setting some rules down so [that I] won’t go back to that person.

What have the artists said about the song?
Genius Answer

Well, it’s a sassy number. It’s definitely one of the crazier ones. Like, I feel like when the beat drops, it’s very different to some of the stuff on the album, but somehow it works and it fits in. It’s the kind of thing that you go up to your friends and you tell them, like, ‘Don’t go back to that man, here are some rules.’ You’re always way better at giving your mates advice rather than yourself. This is me taking on my own problems and actually setting myself some rules for not going back to this guy who keeps messing me about.
