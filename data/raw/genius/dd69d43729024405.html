“Shoop” peaked at #4 on the Billboard Hot 100 and topped Billboard’s Hot Rap Singles chart. It also peaked at #3 on Billboard’s R&B chart.

The song samples The Sweet Inspirations' song “I’m Blue.”

Is this song related to Whitney Houston's „Exhale (Shoop Shoop)” in any way?
Genius Answer

Whitney Houston came out with her “Exhale (Shoop Shoop)” after hearing our “Shoop.” She came out with her song. And she said that she wanted a song like “Shoop.” She actually said that.

source: RollingStone
