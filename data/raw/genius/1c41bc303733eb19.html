About
Song Bio

“Let Me Love You” is Biebs' ode to a girl who seems ready to put an end to their relationship. Over smooth tropical production from DJ Snake, Bieber pleads with his lover to stick around and receive his love. This subject matter has become a popular topic in Justin’s music as seen on 2015’s Purpose and his feature on Major Lazer’s 2016 hit “Cold Water.”

Who is "Let Me Love You" about?
Genius Answer

The song is rumored to be about Justin’s ex-flame Selena Gomez, who teased the idea that she would be alongside DJ Snake and Justin Bieber after lip-syncing to a song with a similar chorus.

Is "Let Me Love You" Justin's first feature of 2016?
Genius Answer

No, just weeks before this song was released, Justin was featured on Major Lazer’s “Cold Water.”
