About
Song Bio

“Thunder” is the second single from Imagine Dragons' third album, following “Believer”.

“Thunder” is about not conforming to society’s standards. Dan sings about being told what to do as a child, and that he can’t follow his dreams. In the second verse, he says that he overcame everyone that told him what to do, and achieved his dreams of being on stage.

“Thunder” peaked at #4 on Billboard’s Hot 100, and scored the band their first song to surpass 1,000,000,000 (one billion) views on YouTube.

What did Imagine Dragons say about "Thunder"?
Genius Answer

‘Thunder’ is: ‘I’m so happy for a really (crappy) middle school and high school existence and getting kicked out of college.‘ It’s reflecting on all those things and saying, ‘Good, I’m happy for all that because that brought me to this place of being. It created angst inside of me that bred art.’

–Dan Reynolds via Las Vegas Review-Journal

How many times do they say thunder? (Including Background)
Genius Answer

Over 70 times, but dedicated fans have estimated it’s 76 times, give or take 1 or 2 thunders

How did the song gain popularity?
Genius Answer

Similar to how “Believer” gained popularity before releasing onto public radio, Thunder gained widespread traction when Microsoft used the song to advertise their new Surface Laptop.
