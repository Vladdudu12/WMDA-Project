About
Song Bio

Swae Lee, Slim Jxmmi, Mike WiLL Made-It, and Gucci Mane team up on a track comparing themselves to the legendary English rock band The Beatles. The three rappers address their fame, women, and, most importantly, their money.

A month after its release as a Sremmlife 2 single “Black Beatles” gained popularity when it became affiliated with the “Mannequin Challenge,” a viral video sensation that captures people standing still as still as a mannequin while performing various acts. Sports teams, celebrities, schools, and even Rae Sremmurd themselves have participated in this challenge.


On November 14, 2016, it was revealed that the track had “rode the #MannequinChallenge” to the No. 1 spot on the Billboard Hot 100 chart. It was both Rae Sremmurd’s and Gucci Mane’s first No. 1 single on the Hot 100.

How did Gucci Mane end up on this song?
Verified Commentary
Mike WiLL Made-It
Genius Answer

The beat was just an idea. I had bought this new laptop and then the first beat I had made on it was “Black Beatles” and I sent it to Swae Lee like, “Man here’s an idea I came up with the other day.”

He did “Black Beatles” immediately and sent it back like, “Yo tell me what you think about this. It’s a hook I just laid on there.” I’m like “Yo, this shit hard!”

At the time I was over at Gucci’s house, so I was like, “Gucci, you know we gotta have you on SremmLife 2.” And then he was like “I wanna be on there.”

I played him the song with Swae Lee on the hook and he was like “Yeah this shit a banger. I’m gonna hop on it.” Gucci hopped on it and then I let Jxmmi hear the whole thing and Jxmmi knocked it out right away.

Swae sent me like two verses and we ended up like combining two verses together and that’s how we got “Black Beatles”.
