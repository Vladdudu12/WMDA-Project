About
Song Bio

“Numb” is not only one of Linkin Park’s most recognizable and emotionally charged tracks, but also one of their most commercially successful.

Written just a week before the band entered the studio, the song quickly became a defining moment for Linkin Park, both musically and emotionally. Frontman Chester Bennington channeled his own turbulent youth and struggles into the performance, delivering a vocal that captured feelings of exhaustion, alienation, and the desire to break free from others' expectations.

“It’s very recognizable as our sound,” Mike Shinoda explained in a 2003 interview.

I thinks it was a nice way to end the album because it kind of sums up the record. It’s very recognizable as our sound. It sounds like a Linkin Park song but it does have some mood that Meteora has if that makes any sense. Maybe I can say that better. When you hear it, you can easily recognize it as a Linkin Park song but it obviously belongs on Meteora. It obviously belongs in this new group of songs just because of the way the tone of the song is and the lyrics are.

The lyrics of “Numb” resonated deeply with listeners, especially those who felt trapped by the expectations of parents or authority figures. The song tells the story of someone who feels unable to live up to those expectations and would rather forge their own path, a theme that made it an anthem for a generation struggling with identity and pressure. The music video, filmed in Prague, visually amplifies the song’s message. It follows a young outcast artist as she navigates feelings of isolation and hopelessness. Scenes of her being ignored and bullied at school, as well as her self-harm scars, powerfully depict the pain behind the lyrics.

Commercially, “Numb” is one of Linkin Park’s most successful singles. It topped the US Billboard Modern Rock Tracks (now known as the Alternative Airplay) chart for 12 weeks, spent three weeks at number one on the Mainstream Rock Tracks (now known as Mainstream Rock Airplay) chart , and peaked at number 11 on the Billboard Hot 100. The song was certified 4× Platinum by the RIAA, selling over 4 million copies in the United States alone, and has surpassed 1 billion streams on Spotify. Internationally, it also achieved significant chart success, including top 10 placements in 10 different countries.

After Chester Bennington’s tragic passing in 2017, “Numb” took on even greater significance for fans and the band. Jay-Z reflected on Bennington’s struggles, saying:

I really think that hopefully his death serves as a wake up call. Mental health is a real thing. You never know that people are going through and you think that because they’re performers and he’s sold millions of records… that doesn’t equate to happiness… A lot of people, we don’t deal with what’s happening to us, we just keep going. Especially for a performer like that, you just start numbing yourself. You become numb. He’s singing it, he’s telling you become numb. Hopefully his death wakes a lot of people up and a lot of people start taking care of themselves.

Twenty years after its release, “Numb” remains a powerful and cathartic anthem, resonating with new generations and standing as a testament to Linkin Park’s emotional honesty and musical legacy.
