About
Song Bio

“Toxic” is a dance-pop anthem that depicts a dangerously addictive love. It serves as the second single for Britney Spears' fourth album, In The Zone. To this day, it remains Spears' biggest hit. 

Cathy Dennis confirmed that she wrote it with Janet Jackson in mind and that it took seven days to finish. It was originally handed to Kylie Minogue, who wasn’t sure about having a song with that title. In Spears' hands, it reached the Billboard charts, including #1 on Pop Airplay, #4 on Dance/Electronic Streaming Songs, and #9 on the Hot 100. It earned Spears a Grammy Award for Best Dance Recording and has been certified six times platinum by the RIAA. 

The bhangra influence is evident from the sampled Bollywood strings—a fusion of two sections from Lata Mangeshkar and S. P. Balasubrahmanyam’s “Tere Mere Beech Mein." The instrumentation also consists of a surf guitar with an aggressive twang. The result is a cinematic atmosphere similar to a spy film. 

An iconic pop culture moment, the music video displays some of Spears' most memorable looks, later serving as inspiration for Taylor Swift’s “Bad Blood” visuals. Spears has performed the track on numerous occasions, such as 2003’s ABC Special, the Apple Music Festival, and the 2016 Billboard Music Awards with a medley of her hits. 

In 2023, “Toxic” became Spears' first billion-streaming song, introducing her to the Spotify Billions Club. In addition, it found new life on the Billboard Hot 100 with Altego’s “Toxic Pony” and Jamieson Shaw’s “Toxic Las Vegas.”

What did Britney Spears say about "Toxic"?
Genius Answer

In 2003 Brit told MTV:

“It’s basically about a girl addicted to a guy. "I really like ‘Toxic.’ It’s an upbeat song. It’s really different, that’s why I like it so much. This villain girl, she’ll do anything to get what she wants. She goes through different obstacles.”

Was 'Toxic' offered to anyone else before Britney recorded the song?
Genius Answer

Yes! Kylie Minogue revealed that the song was offered to her first, but that she rejected the song.
