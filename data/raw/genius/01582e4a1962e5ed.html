About
Song Bio

This song is the first single from D12’s second album D12 World. The song has a satirical tone, suggesting Eminem is the lead singer of the “band”.

The music video parodies other music videos, including 50 Cent’s “In Da Club”, Eminem’s “Superman” and “Lose Yourself” videos, as well as Kelis’s “Milkshake”.
