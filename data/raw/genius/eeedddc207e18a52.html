About
Verified Bio

“Needed Me” is a mid-tempo mood piece with raw lyrics set over top of dark fluttery synth sounds produced by DJ Mustard. Rihanna provides a solid female empowerment anthem with this one (per her fans on Twitter).

It’s basically 3 minutes and 12 seconds of Rihanna serving tea.

This song was cowritten by singer-songwriter Starrah—who’s worked with Jeremih, Kid Ink, and G-Eazy—and Prince Charlez—who’s written for Usher, Enrique Iglesias, and the Backstreet Boys.

What did Rihanna say about "Needed Me"?
Genius Answer

Writer Starrah told Billboard:

The way it translated to other people was a good feeling for me. It changed my perspective on my ability to write a universal song. I dated a girl who told me that I helped her get out of her old relationship with that song… That’s crazy.

How did the song do on the charts?
Genius Answer

“Needed Me” peaked at #7 on the Billboard Hot 100 chart for the chart week of July 2, 2016. It spent a total 45 weeks on the chart, and has become Rihanna’s longest-charting hit.
