About
Song Bio

This Colombian Cha-Cha-style song includes the Madrigal Family warning Mirabel not to mention her uncle Bruno, who has the gift of telling prophecy. Since his sometimes troubling prophecies come true, he is the mysterious outcast of the family.
It’s also the first Disney song since “A Whole New World” to reach the first position on the Billboard Hot 100, becoming Disney’s most recent hit.

What did Carolina Gaitán, Mauro Castillo, Adassa, Rhenzy Feliz, Diane Guerrero & Stephanie Beatriz say about "We Don't Talk About Bruno"?
Genius Answer

Lin-Manuel has stated in an interview:

It’s a complicated family number and it introduces themes from characters that we don’t have time to go into with their own song. I looked to ‘A Weekend in the Country’ from ‘A Little Night Music,’ and ‘It’s Beginning to Snow’ from ‘Rent.’ We’ve got lots of different characters going in different directions.

Does the track have any certifications?
Genius Answer

Yes, “We Don’t Talk About Bruno” was certified 4x platinum on March 17, 2023.
