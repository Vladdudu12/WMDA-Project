Song Bio

“Play” peaked at #18 on the Billboard Hot 100 and #2 on Billboard’s dance music chart. Christina Milian was one of the co-writers on the song and she provided backing vocals on it as well.

Q&A

Find answers to frequently asked questions about the song and explore its deeper meaning

Did the single receive any certifications?
Genius Answer

“Play” was certified silver in the United Kingdom after selling 200,000 units. The single also went gold in Australia and gold in Belgium.

What did Christina Milian say about this song?
Genius Answer

Hands down, [J.Lo] killed it. She’s so good. I love that song. And I couldn’t believe at 19 years old I wrote a song for J.Lo. I went to bed late that night, woke up the next morning and had to go to the studio. I had the track and I didn’t write anything [to it]. So I sat down in the bed, I had this track, I was really tired and I played the track and I was like, ‘This just sounds too easy.’ It was just too easy to write at that moment. I wrote the song in, like, 15 minutes and it was a joke to me because I had just started to become a writer. […] The guy comes in and he hears ‘Play,’ loves it and is like, ‘I want this as her single.’ And the next thing I know, she’s in New York, she’s recording the song and I came in to help rewrite some of the stuff. It’s funny when people talk about this being kind of a thing about me singing on the song with Jennifer. I mean, I have background singers on some of my songs. It’s no different than Michael Jackson having background singers on songs or Britney Spears. This is what music is made of. You want a blend of voices. It makes songs better, to me.

-via Page Six
