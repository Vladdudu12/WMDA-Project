About
Song Bio

“Starships” is about defying expectations and realizing your potential—a lesson that’s very close to Nicki Minaj’s heart.

This track saw Nicki Minaj make her big transition from urban crossover rapper to mainstream pop songstress. With a little help from producer/songwriter RedOne, the baddest bitch takes her “Super Bass” success to the next level.

How well did the song do on the Billboard Charts?
Genius Answer

“Starships” was a massive hit for Minaj. It’s one of her longest charting singles on the Hot 100, and spent much of its run inside the top 10 of the chart, peaking at #5. The song spent a total of 31 total weeks on the Hot 100. chart. It also hit #2 on both Dance related charts, and #3 on US Rhythmic.

What certifications has this track received?
Genius Answer

On September 19, 2023, “Starships” was certified 9x platinum by the RIAA.
