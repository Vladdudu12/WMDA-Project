About
Song Bio

“On the Floor” is a song recorded by American singer Jennifer Lopez for her seventh studio album, LOVE?. The song features guest vocals from American rapper Pitbull. The song was released by as the lead single from the album and became a massive success worldwide.

The song incorporates Latin beats with “Llorando Se Fue,” popularized by Kaoma’s 1989 hit single “Lambada” – the “Forbidden Dance”

Q&A

Find answers to frequently asked questions about the song and explore its deeper meaning

How did "On the Floor" chart?
Genius Answer

“On the Floor” went #1 in a number of countries, including Australia, Canada, France, Spain and the U.K.

In the United States, “On the Floor” peaked at #3 on Billboard’s Hot 100 chart, and #1 on Billboard’s Dance Club Songs chart.

What certifications did the single receive?
Genius Answer

“On the Floor” is certified 3x platinum by the RIAA.

The single is also certified platinum in the U.K. and has amassed over 2.5 million streams in Denmark.

What have the artists said about the music video?
Genius Answer

….let’s talk “On The Floor”…this was my first track after I had my babies and I hadn’t put out a record in a few years. They gave us no money to make this video. It ended up leaking a few days before my debut on “American Idol” and started climbing straight up the charts! It’s also my first video to reach 1 billion views on YouTube, and I was the first female artist to do that.
