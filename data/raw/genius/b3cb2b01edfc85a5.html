About
Song Bio

This song was Icona Pop and Charli XCX’s breakout song and first hit in the US. Charli wrote this song and contributed vocals to the track. Icona Pop’s producer Patrik Berger (the Swedish producer behind Robyn’s “Dancing On My Own”) was in the studio with them recording their song “Good For You” when he first played them Charli’s demo. They immediately fell in love. Icona Pop’s Caroline Hjelt remembered in an interview with CMJ:

It was her singing over a cute beat. And we were like, ‘Wait, wait, wait, wait. What’s that?’ We were going through some love drama, and we could really relate to the lyric…We took it to Style Of Eye and told him, ‘This is the feeling that we want on it. We want the punkiness. We want the ‘fuck it’ feeling. We want everything that we felt when we were singing it.’ So it came out totally different.

The song was a huge success for singer Charli XCX who would later go on to feature on another chart topping song, “Fancy” with rapper Iggy Azalea.

I Love It by Icona Pop was released May 6th, 2012. It gained prominence when it was featured in the “cocaine dance club” scene on the HBO show Girls.

What did Icona Pop say about "I Love It"?
Genius Answer

On why the song took long to gain popularity:

In the beginning, we were only supposed to release it in Sweden. Then the bloggers started to spread it and suddenly people in Australia were craving it. Then America jumped on that train, so we were so very busy. In the UK, you have Radio 1 and that’s your big station, but in America you have 30,000 radio stations. So we’ve been basically going to every station and saying, ‘hey, this is a really good song – you should put it on, because otherwise you’ll regret it!’"

– Via BBC (2013)

What has the media said about the song?
Genius Answer

In 2018, NPR ranked this as the #73 greatest song by a female or nonbinary artist in the 21st century, saying:

Jumping into the air, party people around the world yell at the top of their lungs: ‘I don’t care! I love it!’ Written by British musician Charli XCX, who recorded the song with Swedish duo Icona Pop, this cathartic breakup anthem rules the dance floor. Edgy club beats kick the intro in, segueing boldly into pop romp vocals with punk attitude. Icona Pop is made up of Caroline Hjelt and Aino Jawo, Stockholm musicians who throw a lot of parties together; they formed in 2009 and specialize in songs about ‘being angry but sounding happy.’ With its unique sound and impressive reach, “I Love It” continues to be influential as it is appropriated and remixed into hip-hop and DJ culture by artists as diverse as Chiddy Bang, DJ Earworm and Style of Eye.

Does this track have any RIAA certifications?
Genius Answer

Yes! “I Love It” has since been certified 5x platinum as of December 14th, 2022.
