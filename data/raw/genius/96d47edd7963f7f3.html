About
Song Bio

The first single off Carter IV features rap aristocrat Cory Gunz & Lil Wayne yearning to go home (Harry Belafonte style..)

In the House of Detention for Men, the oldest jail on Rikers Island, each inmate’s cell is 6 feet by 8 feet by 7 feet.

Wayne flexes on a rather minimalistic beat.

What's going on in the song's visuals?
Genius Answer

The visuals open with a recreation of a scene from Christopher Nolan’s 2010 film, Inception.

Multiple women In red dresses walk through a green-screened cityscape during the visuals. This is an allusion to the 1999 film, The Matrix, where the “Woman in Red” is a simulated character created to distract.
