About
Song Bio

First released on December 30, 2015 via SoundCloud, “Look At Me” quickly grew notorious before receiving a second surge in popularity in late January 2017, when Drake previewed a song eventually released as “KMT,” which had a similar sounding flow.

After hearing the preview for “KMT,” X tweeted his frustration at the similarities. Drake denied stealing the flow, though he admitted there were similarities, prompting X to respond from jail, saying he would rather a big name artist like Drake help with his legal situation, rather than steal his flow and not give any credit.

Subsequently, in May 2017, “Look At Me!” was edited for better sound quality and placed on his Revenge mixtape, giving it a wider release on streaming services.

The controversial music video for “Look At Me!” released on September 12, 2017.

How did this song come together?
Verified Commentary
Rojas On The Beat
Genius Answer

One day me and X were sitting at the crib going through beats and he wasn’t really rocking with any of then, and then the last one I showed him was that beat, and he was like, “Rojas, this is it!”

In 15 minutes, it was done. X already knew the whole song in his head before he made it. He’s creative as fuck. X decided to distort the whole track in one.

What's the origin of the beat?
Verified Commentary
Rojas On The Beat
Genius Answer

We made it at Jimmy Duval’s Studio. The beat was actually made for RetcH, when he came down to Miami after he had a show in Tampa.

He was staying at Jimmy’s crib after I introduced them, when me and Jimmy decided to work on some stuff for Retch. That’s when we made the “Look At Me!” beat. We never got to send it to Retch because he went to jail and I lost contact with him and his management.

How did they find the sample?
Verified Commentary
Rojas On The Beat
Genius Answer

A couple months before I sampled it, I was listening to a song by Young Roddy called “Water” and I was fucking with him heavy. I thought the sample was really hard and I wanted to use it but i failed with it so many times before I got it right.
