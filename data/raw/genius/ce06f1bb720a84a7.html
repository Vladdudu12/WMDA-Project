About
Song Bio

On “Wants and Needs,” Drake and Lil Baby go back and forth on aspects of life involved in the balance of what is wanted versus what is needed.

Between women, money, inner-circles, religion, and more, both rappers have to balance the importance of many things in life.

Have Drake and Lil Baby collaborated on a song before?
Genius Answer

Yes, this marks their fourth official collaboration, but it is only their third formally. Their previous unions are outlined below:

  • Baby, “Yes Indeed” (May 2018)
  • Baby & Gunna, “Never Recover” (Oct. 2018)
  • Future, “Life Is Good (Remix)” (Feb. 2020)
Have Drake and Cardo collaborated before?
Genius Answer

Yes, this marks their sixth collaboration, following these tracks:

  • The Game, “100” (Jun. 2015)
  • Drake, “Grammys” (Apr. 2016)
  • Drake, “God’s Plan” (Jan. 2018)
  • Drake, “Landed” (May 2020)
  • Drake, “Laugh Now Cry Later” (August 2020)
Have Drake and Dez Wright collaborated before?
Genius Answer

Yes, this marks their second collaboration, following Drake’s May 2020 song, “Landed.”
