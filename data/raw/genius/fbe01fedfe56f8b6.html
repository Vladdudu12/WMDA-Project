About
Song Bio

London in the 80s was divided (and still is) into the wealthy West End and the more down-at-heel, East End.

The major entertainment centre of London is based around the West End and us boys used to hang around there in the hopes of landing a West End girl in an effort to social climb.

This song was featured on GTA V’s in-game radio station, “Non-Stop Pop”. The majority of the sounds are produced by the E-mu Emulator II synthesizer.
