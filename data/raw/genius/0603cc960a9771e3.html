About
Song Bio

Rihanna returns from a music hiatus with “Lift Me Up,” the lead single from the Black Panther: Wakanda Forever movie soundtrack, titled Wakanda Forever: The Album released in 2022. This marks Rihanna’s first solo release since her eighth album ANTI in January 2016. Rihanna announced the song via Twitter two days before its release, after Marvel teased it the day before.

The record was written alongside Ludwig Göransson and Ryan Coogler, who composed the movie’s score and directed the film, respectively, as well as Nigerian rising artist Tems, as a tribute to the late Chadwick Boseman, who played King T’Challa, the protagonist on the original Black Panther.

On January 24, 2023, it was revealed that “Lift Me Up” was one of the nominees for the 95th Academy Awards' “Best Original Song” award.

What have the artists said about the song?
Genius Answer

Chadwick Boseman passed away in 2020 from colon cancer. His passing shocked the world after he became a figure for black people all around the globe since playing King T'Challa/Black Panther. His sudden loss marked the cast, crew and everyone involved in the films. Tems, one of the writers of the track, opens up with Variety about how his passing inspired the lead track for the film sung by Rihanna.

After speaking with Ryan and hearing his direction for the film and the song, I wanted to write something that portrays a warm embrace from all the people that I’ve lost in my life. I tried to imagine what it would feel like if I could sing to them now and express how much I miss them, Rihanna has been an inspiration to me so hearing her convey this song is a great honor.

– Tems for Variety
