About
Song Bio

“Until It Sleeps” is the first single and the fourth song from Metallica’s 1996 album Load. It was the band’s first number one song on the US Billboard Hot Mainstream Rock Tracks chart, as well as their first and only song to hit the top 10 of the Billboard Hot 100, peaking at number 10.

The lyrics of the song, written by Hetfield, are about his mother’s battle with cancer, to which the ‘it’ in the title refers. The pain in the song can refer to both his own emotional pain and his mother’s physical pain.
