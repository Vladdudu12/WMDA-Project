About
Song Bio

“What Lovers Do” is the first collaboration between Maroon 5 and SZA, and the second collaboration between Maroon 5 and the TDE label, following 2016’s “Don’t Wanna Know” with Kendrick Lamar. The track centers around a poker metaphor on the chorus.

Maroon 5 gave fans just a two-day notice that they would be releasing the song, along with a #WhatLoversDo social media campaign that enabled fans to hear a preview of the single before it was released.

Who is SZA?
Genius Answer

SZA is an R&B/neo soul singer-songwriter from New Jersey who signed to Kendrick Lamar’s record label, Top Dawg Entertainment, in 2013.

In June 2017, she released her debut studio album, Ctrl, accompanied by the hit singles “The Weekend” and the Travis Scott-assistant “Love Galore”.
