About
Song Bio

This is the remix of Harlow’s January 2020 hit single “WHATS POPPIN,” which, prior to the release of the remix, reached #16 on the Billboard Hot 100.

Harlow first announced the remix a day before its release on Instagram, revealing the features included on the remix along with its cover art.

What did Jack Harlow say about "WHATS POPPIN (Remix)"?
Verified Commentary
Jack Harlow
via Apple Music
Genius Answer

Getting DaBaby and Tory Lanez on the record is very exciting, but Lil Wayne is a dream come true. Something I can say was done for the place I’m from, a moment my city and state can be proud of.

How have these artists previously collaborated?
Genius Answer

This is both Harlow and DaBaby’s first collaboration with each other and with either of the other featured artists. However, his is Tory Lanez’s fourth collaboration with Lil Wayne:

  • Tory & Rich The Kid’s “TAlk tO Me (Remix)” (Nov. 2018)
  • Tory’s “Thoughts” (Nov. 2019)
  • Wayne’s “Help (Funeral)” (May 2020)
