About
Song Bio

A classic club and love song all about getting that special someone back to the crib and getting hot and heavy, the lyrics are fueled with sexual innuendo. Such innuendos including, but not limited to: “candy shop”, “lollipop”, “hit the spot”, “wanna taste”, “magic stick”, and the most popular “I melt in your mouth girl, not in your hand”.

Easily one of his most recognizable songs, 50 Cent’s “Candy Shop” topped the Billboard Hot 100 and was nominated for a Grammy in the Best Rap Song category.

What did 50 Cent say about "Candy Shop"?
Genius Answer

I think the song itself is great. I attempted to be as sexual as possible, from a male perspective, without being vulgar or obscene. I think I did a great job on it. The video itself, visually, me and Jessy Terrrero [the director] came up with the treatment for. Trick Daddy had a song [“Sugar”] that he put out where he had candy references in the video, so I didn’t go that route, and we tried to do something a little different."

—50 Cent

Has this track received any certifications?
Genius Answer

“Candy Shop” was certified platinum by the RIAA on June 14, 2006.
