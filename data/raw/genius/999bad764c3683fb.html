About
Song Bio

“Beautiful” is a 2003 single by Snoop Dogg from his album Paid Tha Cost To Be Da Bo$$.

This single peaked at #6 on the U.S. Hot 100 chart, and was successful in other countries such as Australia and New Zealand, where it peaked at number four.
