About
Song Bio

J. Cole’s first offering of 2019 serves as a warning in advance of an extensive rollout of Dreamville material. On the track, Cole envisions himself in the center of the old and new generations of hip-hop, therefore making him the “middle child” of rap.

“Middle Child Syndrome” is a psychological condition in which middle children sometimes feel neglected by their families due to the attention that their younger and older siblings receive.

How was the song teased ahead of its release?
Genius Answer

On January 21, 2019, Cole purged his entire Instagram. He then proceeded to post and delete several phrases, which turned out to compose the beginning of the song’s refrain.

Later that day, he announced that the song would be released on Janaury 23, 2019, specifically at 9PM.

On the following day, Cole posted a video of First Choice’s 1973 song “Wake Up to Me.” That song’s trumpet progression is sampled throughout this song’s instrumental.

Thirty minutes before the song’s release, Cole took to Instagram and Twitter to post numerous images of true middle children, including Michael Jordan, Bill Gates, Madonna, John F. Kennedy, LiAngelo Ball, Britney Spears, and Jennifer Lopez.

When did Cole first perform this song live?
Genius Answer

During the 2019 NBA All-Star Game Halftime Performance, Cole performed a rendition of “MIDDLE CHILD” for the first time, along with his verse on 21 Savage’s “a lot,” his April 2018 track, “ATM,” and his December 2014 tracks, “Love Yourz” and “No Role Modelz.”

Has T-Minus worked with J. Cole before?
Genius Answer

Yes, T-Minus produced J. Cole’s April 2018 song “Kevin’s Heart” and 6LACK’s September 2018 song “Pretty Little Fears,” which features Cole.
