About
Song Bio

On an emotionally raw mix of R&B and trap, “Don’t” is about how he wishes to be with a girl who is mistreated by her man. The song later served as the lead single on Bryson’s debut album T R A P S O U L.

The song was a pinnacle of Bryson’s fresh R&B career, receiving over 100 million plays on his SoundCloud in under a year. After the increased popularity of the single, he acknowledged that he created it in his living room in a now-deleted Instagram post:

wow this is crazy. I made this shit in my living room

“Don’t” peaked at #13 on the Billboard Hot 100 chart. Making it one of his highest-charting singles in his career. And as well as Bryson’s first top 20 hit.

Bryson reflected on the single in 2024 with Complex saying he nearly deleted the song, a friend called to inform him that girls on campus liked it and encouraged him to re-upload it:

It was one of those situations where I was just in my head, […] I had played it for, like, five people, and they all gave me like just ‘eh’ type responses. So I was like, damn. Maybe this shit sucks. But then I was just like, man, but I’ve been listening to it over and over and over and over. It has to be good a little bit. It was moving really slow, […] Nobody really cared. Nobody said anything. None of my friends hit me up. So I deleted it. I was like, fuck this shit. What am I doing?.

What did Bryson Tiller say about "Don't"?
Genius Answer

In an interview with NME Tiller said:

I bought a bunch of stuff on eBay and it all came in the mail one day. I woke up one morning and it was the first song I recorded on my new equipment. I did it before my daughter woke up.

He added:

My computer messed up and every time I tried to record there was just a bunch of static, the song was supposed to be longer but I had to cut it short because I couldn’t record anything else.

Tiller told fuse.tv that really, the song was about his own relationship:

It wasn’t even really about me taking somebody’s girl or trying to tell some girl she’d be better off, it was really about me. I wrote it from the perspective of me, someone who wasn’t doing what I needed to be doing in my relationship. I was like, wow, what if someone as serious as me comes in and talks to her [my girl], and be like, “What’s he doing? You deserve better.”

Who is King Vory?
Genius Answer

King Vory is a Houston-based artist who has collaborated with Bryson Tiller before, on his track “Break Bread.”

Has this track received any certifications?
Genius Answer

“Don’t” was certified 7x platinum by the RIAA on November 23, 2021.
