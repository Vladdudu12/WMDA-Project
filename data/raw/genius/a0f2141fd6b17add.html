About
Verified Bio

Nicki goes big—literally—with a song about butts, sex, and more butts, backed by a “Baby Got Back” sample, nonetheless.

“Anaconda” is the 2nd single for and 12th track on Nicki Minaj’s third studio album, The Pinkprint, and it’s loaded to the brim with sexual innuendo.

The single’s artwork caused quite some controversy over the brazen imagery of her bare tuckus.

What did Nicki Minaj say about "Anaconda"?
Verified Commentary
Nicki Minaj
via Complex
Genius Answer

I wanted to create a song that embraced curvy women. I wanted to be sexual but be playful with it. And I wanted it to be so melodic that even if you don’t understand English you could still go along with the melody and you would have no idea about all the raunchy shit I’m saying—I get a kick out of that. It was simple to write. I just created the melody and then I let the words happen. I started laughing when I said, “Boy toy named Troy.” [Laughs]


That whole song, I was just being dumb. It was a joke. My biggest thing was seeing how my girlfriends Sherika and Thembi were going to react. If they don’t like a song, they’ll be like, “No.” As soon as they walked in the studio, we were laughing and having fun. I thought, if we’re doing this, then everybody is going to have fun with it.

What did the producer say about the song?
Verified Commentary
AnonXmous
Genius Answer

The track idea was conceived on a hot day in Miami, in which a good friend of mine’s phone was on shuffle and it played “Baby Got Back” By Sir Mix A lot. I instantly thought, “this is has never been flipped, and Nicki Minaj would be perfect for it. In obvious ways.” so I sampled a line from an old song of hers so it could have a hook already on it. After masterminding with Big bro and mentor Polow Da Don as well as, Super Producers Da Interns, History was made. Missy Never Had or was meant for this.

What makes the song important?
Genius Answer

“Anaconda” is Nicki Minaj’s highest charting single to date, spending 16 weeks on the Billboard Hot 100 and peaking at number 2. The track was nominated for best Rap Song at the 2015 Grammys, was nominated for “Best Female Video” and won the “Best Hip Hop Video” in the 2015 MTV Music Video Awards.

“Anaconda” broke Vevo’s 24 hour streaming record, wracking up over 19 Million plays. Her record was upended by Taylor Swift’s “Bad Blood” in 2015.

The track spurred media controversy for its lewd imagery and sexually provocative content, spurring the Huffington Post to ask if the track is “too racy for its own good?.”
