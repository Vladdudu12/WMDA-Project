About
Song Bio

“Touch Me, Tease Me” centers around a boastful woman and man who’s into her “loving ways”. The song is on Case’s self-titled debut album, and the soundtrack to the 1996 film, The Nutty Professor.

Featuring rapper Foxy Brown and singer Mary J. Blige (who at the time was Case’s girlfriend), the single peaked at #14 on the Billboard Hot 100 while only being on the chart for 20 weeks. In addition, the single was also certified Gold by the RIAA.
