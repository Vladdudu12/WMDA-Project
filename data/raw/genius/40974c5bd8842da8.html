About
Song Bio

“Party Rock Anthem” is the first single from LMFAO’s sophomore and final album, Sorry for Party Rocking. Its electronic, club-friendly production and catchy chorus propelled it to #1 on the Billboard Hot 100, becoming LMFAO’s first of two singles to top the chart.
