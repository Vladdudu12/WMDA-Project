About
Song Bio

“Always on my Mind” by Johnny Christopher, Mark James and Wayne Carson appears on the 1982 Willie Nelson album of the same name.

This song won Country Music Association’s Song of the Year and Single of the Year awards as well as the Grammy Awards for Best Male Country Vocal Performance, Best Country Song, Song of the Year in 1982, and is in CMT’s 500 Greatest Country Songs.

This song is included in the Country Genius – Male and Female Perspectives on Love and Sex. It shows how difficult it is for some men to show their true feelings, and to understand what a woman wants from a man.

What certifications has the single received?
Genius Answer

“Always On My Mind” was certified Platinum by the RIAA on October 7, 1991.

How did the song chart on Billboard?
Genius Answer

It peaked at #1 on 5.8.1982 for two weeks on the Hot Country Songs Chart. It spent 21 weeks on the chart.

It also peaked at #2 on the Billboard 200, #2 on Adult Contemporary, and #5 on the Hot 100.
