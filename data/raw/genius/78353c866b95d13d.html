Q&A

Find answers to frequently asked questions about the song and explore its deeper meaning

Was Pharrell involved in producing this song, or was it just Calvin Harris?
Genius Answer

Pharrell Williams did some additional vocal production, but the beat itself was solely produced by Calvin Harris. Pharrell also has vocals and writing credits on the song, of course.

Has a second version of the music video for "Feels" been released?
Genius Answer

A second version of the video was officially released on August 27th, 2017.

Does this track have any RIAA certifications?
Genius Answer

Yes. “Feels” was certified 3x platinum by the RIAA on October 26, 2020.
