What did Spice Girls say about "Spice Up Your Life"?
Genius Answer

Producer and co-writer Biff Stannard told publication Music Week:

We were talking about Bollywood films, the colours and how the Spice Girls could present themselves. It was a matter of how do we get everything in to one song?"

Does an alternate version of the music video exist?
Genius Answer

Yes. To coincide with the anniversary of their Spiceworld album, the Spice Girls released an alternate version of the “Spice Up Your Life” music video on October 13, 2022. The video features previously unseen footage not found in the original music video.
