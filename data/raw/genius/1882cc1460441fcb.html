About
Song Bio

“We Paid,” the second collaborative track between Baby and 42 Dugg on My Turn, features the two rappers flexing their money, cars, and newfound fame from the rap industry.

The track was first previewed by Baby in an Instagram Live teasing the deluxe version of My Turn. On April 9, 2020, Baby uploaded a clip previewing the song’s music video.

How did this track perform on the Billboard charts?
Genius Answer

“We Paid” debuted at #61 on the Billboard Hot 100 during the chart week of May 16, 2020.

During the chart week of July 11, 2020, “We Paid” peaked at #10 on the chart. This is Dugg’s first Top 10 song on the chart.
