About
Song Bio

Collaboratively written by the Pointer Sisters and Trevor Lawrence, and led by sister Anita Pointer, the song was originally recorded for, and appeared on, the Sisters' 1982 album So Excited! Upon its initial release, the single was only marginally successful, reaching No. 30 on the Billboard Hot 100.

Two years later, on the group’s RIAA-certified multi-platinum album Break Out, a slightly remixed and edited version of the song was included and re-released as a single. This time, it peaked at No. 9 on the Billboard Hot 100. It is now considered the Sisters' signature song.

In 2001, the song was included in the RIAA and National Endowment for the Arts project Songs of the Century, a list intended to “promote a better understanding of America’s musical and cultural heritage.” The song was ranked #264 out of 365 songs.

On film, the song was featured in Summer Lovers, National Lampoon’s Vacation, Eddie Murphy’s version of The Nutty Professor, and many others. The song was also featured in a memorable episode of the now cult classic TV show Saved by the Bell.
