About
Song Bio

“Ride” is about the journey of life. Throughout the song, Tyler’s lyrics indicate he feels like dying is easy, but finding someone or something to live for is life’s challenge, or “ride.” The song contains heavy reggae influences.

The song also contains callbacks to various twenty one pilots songs, including “Heavydirtysoul,” “Lane Boy,” and “Ode To Sleep.”

What have the artists said about the song?
Genius Answer

During their performance at KROQ’s Almost Acoustic Christmas concert in 2019, Tyler shared a story about the song:

The chorus was just an idea, it was something that i had hidden away. And I showed Jenna [his wife] the song, and I didn’t know if she would even know what I was trying to do with it. And she said “I like that ‘ride’ song”. And I was like “Well, first off, it’s rhyme”. And she said “No, it’s not. It’s ride”. And I said “Okay”. So I finished the song, I recorded it, released it, and It’s become one of my favorite songs that we’ve ever put out.

Does this song utilize any alliterations?
Genius Answer

Yes. Lines like “Pieces of peace in the sun’s peace of mind” make use of heavy alliteration as well as the use of a homophone.

Is there any imagery in this song?
Genius Answer

Yes, there is. Tyler uses the sun, a ride, and bullets as imagery in this song. You can find detailed analyses of each by clicking the links.
