About
Song Bio

“Don’t Lose My Number” is the second single from No Jacket Required. It reached #4 in fall 1985.

Rolling Stone reported:

Phil Collins has no idea what “Don’t Lose My Number” is about. The lyrics came to him spontaneously in the studio and don’t really mean anything, though it’s clear our hero Billy is being pursued by nefarious people and is in quite a bit of danger. Maybe he can call up Rikki from Steely Dan’s “Rikki Don’t Lose That Number” and together they can figure out what the hell is going on.
