About
Song Bio

“Get Low,” the third single from Kings of Crunk, is an anthem both dedicated to and centered around one simple act: the gyration of the female posterior.

Containing a perfect mix of hype-like production, infectious energy, and lyrics that would go on to be referenced by others such as Beyoncé, J. Cole and Jeezy, the club-oriented track became a big breakout moment for the crunk genre reaching the masses, and has remained a staple for all types of gatherings since its release.

Aside from club venues and music charts, it has appeared in the video games Need for Speed: Underground, Def Jam: Icon, Dance Central 3 and Fortnite, the TV shows American Dad and Brooklyn Nine-Nine, several movies (including a rendition by Sandra Bullock in 2009’s The Proposal), and even finding life in a Vine video from the 2010s.
