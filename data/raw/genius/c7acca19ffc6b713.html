About
Song Bio

On “Ric Flair Drip,” Offset flaunts his intimidating and towering wealth. This song is named after 2-time WWE Hall of Famer and 16-World Champion Ric Flair. Offset pays his respects, seeing as he often uses Ric Flair’s trademark shout “Whoo!” as an ad-lib; most notably in “Bad and Boujee”. The two also share a similarly lavish and wild lifestyle.

Since dropping the song, it appears the two have also developed a friendship. They filmed the official music video together, and Offset later wished Ric a happy Birthday via an Instagram post.
