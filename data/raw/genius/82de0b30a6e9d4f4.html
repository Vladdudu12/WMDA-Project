About
Song Bio

“Better Off Alone” is a song released by the Dutch trance music group Alice Deejay. The song was released on July 19, 1998 as the lead single from their debut album, Who Needs Guitars Anyway?

The vocalist on “Better Off Alone” is a Dutch woman called Judith Pronk. She gave up singing a few years after the song came out and now works as a make-up artist.

On her website, Judith says:

I love making people feel beautiful and there for using as less make-up as possible for maximum result. My goal is not to become the world’s best make-up artist, but to let people shine and be the best they are.

What did Alice DeeJay say about "Better Off Alone"?
Genius Answer

I came up with the sentence “Do you think you’re better off alone” when the love of my life had left me. It’s kind of ironic cause I never even told her the song was about her. I never saw her again. It made the emotion of the song as real as it gets. One simple line that said it all. The track was instrumental at first, but I started humming the vocal melody while the track was playing and we decided to add vocals. That turned out to be a wise decision.

Pronti (Sebastiaan Molijn)

Nobody expected “Better Off Alone” to become such a worldwide hit. [DJ] Jurgen wanted to stay the underground club DJ that he was, so we asked Judy to perform the song. It was then that we decided to call it Alice Deejay.

Kalmani (Eelke Kalberg)
